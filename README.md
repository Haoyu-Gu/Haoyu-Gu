# Hi, I'm Haoyu Gu 👋

顾昊瑜 · Generative AI · Discrete representations · Symbolic music

I am a senior undergraduate in Artificial Intelligence at [South China University of Technology](https://www.scut.edu.cn/). In 2027, I will join [Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School](https://www.sigs.tsinghua.edu.cn/) as a master's student, supervised by [Prof. Xiao-Ping Zhang](https://sites.google.com/view/xiaopingzhang/).

My research focuses on **generative AI and discrete representations of structured data**, with current work centered on symbolic music. I study how tokenization affects generation quality, controllability, and editing. I like to approach problems from overlooked angles and rethink how they are defined.

I have collaborated with [Lekai Qian](https://lekai-qian.github.io/) on several projects. At SCUT, I work on symbolic music generation under the guidance of [Prof. Qi Liu](https://drliuqi.github.io/) and on human–computer interaction under the guidance of [Prof. Zhanpeng Jin](https://scuthcc.top/index.php/home-2-2/). I have also received guidance from [Dr. Ziyu Wang](https://zzwaang.github.io/) at New York University on BEAT and [Assoc. Prof. Shuai Wang](https://shuaiwang-nju.github.io/) at Nanjing University on BeatEdit.
